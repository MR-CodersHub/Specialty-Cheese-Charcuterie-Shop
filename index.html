Our Producers

Meet the Traditional Cheesemakers & Curing Masters

We partner directly with independent European family farms that use traditional methods and unhurried time.

Jura Mountains, France

Marcel Petite Cheese Caves

Located inside a historic mountain fortress, these caves remain naturally cool year-round. Master cheesemakers tap every single wheel of Comté with brass hammers to test internal structure and maturity.

Specialty: 36-Month Aged French Comté Cheese
Jabugo, Spain

Jabugo Mountain Bodegas

Set in the oak forests of southern Spain, free-range Iberian pigs feed on sweet wild acorns. Cool mountain night breezes and warm sunny days dry-cure the ham naturally with zero artificial heat.

Specialty: 100% Acorn-Fed Jamón Ibérico
Parma, Italy

Antica Corte Cellars

Dating back to the 14th century along the Po River, the cool morning river fogs and warm afternoons create the ideal climate for curing Culatello ham, renowned as Italy's finest cured meat.

Specialty: Culatello di Zibello Cured Ham
