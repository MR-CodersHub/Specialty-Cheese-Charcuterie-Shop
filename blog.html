Simple Explanations

Gourmet Terms Explained

Clear definitions of terms frequently found on artisanal cheese and charcuterie labels.

Cheese Flavor Crystals

Not salt or grit! These are harmless, crunchy amino-acid crystals (called tyrosine) that naturally form inside cheeses aged 18+ months. They are a sign of premium aging and rich flavor.

Acorn-Fed (Bellota)

The traditional autumn season in Spain when free-range Iberian pigs feed exclusively on oak acorns, giving their cured ham its silky, nutty taste and healthy unsaturated fat.

Natural Cave Rinds

The rustic outer crust formed naturally on aging cheese wheels by cool damp air and beneficial cultures, protecting the creamy paste inside without artificial wax coatings.

Alpine Summer Milk

Milk collected in high mountain pastures during summer while cows graze on wildflowers and herbs. This imparts a rich golden color and aromatic sweet flavor to the cheese.
