The Aging Process

How This Cheese is Made & Aged

The four careful steps that develop its extraordinary flavor and texture.

Months 1–3

1. Salting & Brining

Treated with natural sea salt and rubbed with brine twice weekly on solid spruce wood shelves.

Months 4–12

2. Cool Cave Aging

Moved deep into cool stone cellars where temperature stays between 8°C–12°C with 90% humidity.

Months 13–24

3. Developing Crunchy Crystals

Natural aging creates distinct tiny white flavor crystals (tyrosine), delivering deep savory richness.

Month 36

4. Quality & Sound Inspection

The master cheese maker taps each wheel with a small hammer to ensure no interior cracks and perfect texture.
