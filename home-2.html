Seasonal Harvests

The Two European Seasons of Flavor

Natural dairy and curing fats change with seasonal pasture grass and sweet forest acorns.

Summer Mountain Pastures

Golden Alpine Summer Milk

Cows grazing freely on high mountain wildflowers, clover, and dandelions produce rich, golden milk with remarkable aroma. This creates the nutty sweetness found in our 36-month Comté cheese.

  • ✦ Flavor Notes: Wild mountain honey, roasted hazelnuts, melted butter
  • ✦ Great Wine Pairings: Crisp Chardonnay or dry sparkling white
Shop Alpine Cheeses
Autumn Acorn Harvest

The Spanish Acorn Season

In the oak savannas of southern Spain, free-range Iberian pigs feed on sweet acorns for months. The natural acorn oils soak into the meat, creating healthy marbling that melts smoothly at body temperature.

  • ✦ Flavor Notes: Roasted acorns, wild herbs, savory umami, sweet cured wood
  • ✦ Great Wine Pairings: Spanish Rioja red or dry chilled sherry

Dinner Party Advice

The Three-Course Cheese & Meat Board

How to guide your guests from light, fresh flavors to deep, savory cured meats and rich blue cheeses.

1. The Appetizer
Light, Fresh & Creamy

Start with fresh French goat cheese (Sainte-Maure) and delicate, sweet ribbons of Italian Prosciutto. Delivers a clean, refreshing start to your gathering.

Best Wine: Crisp Sauvignon Blanc or Pinot Grigio
2. The Centerpiece
Nutty, Decadent & Rich

The highlight of your table: 36-Month Aged Comté alongside pure acorn-fed Spanish ham and Black Truffle Triple-Cream Brie.

Best Wine: Sparkling Champagne or Chilled Chardonnay
3. The Grand Finale
Bold, Savory & Lingering

The memorable finish. Authentic French Roquefort Blue Cheese drizzled with sweet honeycomb, paired with Culatello ham and crispy rye crackers.

Best Wine: Sweet Dessert Wine or Port
