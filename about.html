Why We Exist

Our Milestones

A Century of Passion & Quality

How our small family cheese cellar grew into a respected specialty purveyor.

1928

The First Mountain Cheese Cellar

Henri Laurent opened a small stone cellar in France, partnering with four local alpine dairy cooperatives to age traditional cow milk cheese wheels.

1952

Aging in Underground Mountain Fortresses

We secured access to historic underground military fortresses deep inside the French mountains, creating the perfect cool, damp climate for aging Comté cheese for three full years.

1974

Partnering with Spanish Ham Masters

We expanded into cured meats by partnering directly with traditional family bodegas in Jabugo, Spain, committing to 100% acorn-fed Iberian ham sliced exclusively by hand.

2026
Today

Fresh Home Delivery Worldwide

Now bringing these rare, cave-aged European selections and interactive party board tools directly to your kitchen in guaranteed cold packaging.

Where Our Food Comes From

The Three Heritage Regions

Natural climates, pure mountain grass, and centuries of tradition.

Jura & Loire, France

Mountain Caves & Pastures

Home to mountain cows grazing on wildflower meadows at high altitudes and small goat farms along the Loire River valley.

Famous Items: 36-Month Comté, French Goat Cheese Logs, Morbier
Jabugo & Toledo, Spain

Sweet Oak Forests

Ancient oak forests where Iberian pigs roam freely eating sweet acorns, paired with sheep grazing on wild rosemary in central Spain.

Famous Items: 100% Acorn-Fed Jamón Ibérico, Aged Manchego

Where cool breezes from the Italian Alps meet warm sea air in San Daniele, and humid river mists create sweet, tender cured hams.

Famous Items: Prosciutto di San Daniele, Culatello di Zibello
Our Standards

The Four Promises of Quality

Every cheese and meat we deliver conforms to our strict quality principles.

1. Fresh Pure Milk

No factory shortcuts. We preserve the natural floral flavors and rich enzymes from fresh morning pasture milk.

2. Hand-Tapped Quality

Every 40kg cheese wheel is individually struck with an affineur hammer to ensure proper internal structure and crystal formation.

3. Hand-Sliced Ham

We avoid high-speed machine slicers that tear delicate ham fibers. Slicing thin by hand preserves the natural sweet oils.

4. Guaranteed Cold Delivery

From our cellars straight to your home, insulated packaging and frozen gel packs keep your cheeses and meats in prime condition.
