Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information. Last updated October 2026.

1. Information We Collect

When you shop with us, we collect only what we need to serve you well: your name, email address, delivery address, and phone number for orders; your account login details if you register; and your cart contents. We never ask for payment details directly on our pages — payments are processed securely by our payment provider.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Preparing, packing, and cold-shipping your cheese and charcuterie orders.
  • Contacting you about delivery times, delays, or freshness issues.
  • Improving our shop, pairing guides, and board-building tools.
  • Sending newsletters and offers — only if you subscribe (unsubscribe anytime).

3. Delivery Partners & Data Sharing

To deliver your insulated cold boxes, our couriers receive only your name, delivery address, and phone number. We never sell your personal information. Data is shared only with essential service providers (payment processing and shipping) under strict confidentiality.

4. Cookies & On-Device Preferences

Our site remembers simple preferences on your own device — such as light/dark theme, reading direction, and your shopping cart — using your browser's local storage. No advertising trackers are used. You can clear these at any time through your browser settings.

5. Your Rights

You may request a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account and personal data at any time. Simply reach out through our Contact page and we will respond promptly.

Questions About Your Privacy?

Our customer team is happy to help with any privacy request.
