Styling Tips

4 Simple Steps to Arrange a Perfect Board

Easy presentation techniques to make your board look stunning and delicious.

Step 1

1. Place the Cheeses First

Place your main cheeses (such as Comté, Manchego, or Brie) at different corners of the board. Pre-slice 3–4 pieces so guests feel invited to take a slice easily.

Step 2

2. Fold the Cured Meats

Avoid laying meats flat. Fold prosciutto or serrano ham into gentle ribbon folds, or arrange salami slices in overlapping fan shapes so they are easy to lift.

Step 3

3. Add Pickles, Olives & Fruit

Rich cheeses and meats taste best when contrasted with something crisp and tangy. Place small dishes of cornichons, green olives, apple slices, and sweet grapes next to rich cuts.

Step 4

4. Fill Remaining Empty Gaps

A beautiful board looks full and generous. Fill every small empty space with roasted almonds, walnuts, fresh rosemary sprigs, and small jars of truffle honey.

Cutting Techniques

How to Cut & Slice Each Cheese

Proper cutting methods to ensure every slice has the perfect balance of rind and creamy center.

Hard Cheeses (Comté & Manchego)

Never chop just the tip! Cut lengthwise from the outer rind to the tip into slim triangular wedges. This gives each guest both the soft center and the richer edge.

Recommended Tool: Chef's Knife or Cheese Spade

Soft Cheeses (Brie & Camembert)

Cut like a round cake into small pie slices starting from the exact middle. Use a knife with holes in the blade so the soft cream does not stick to the steel.

Recommended Tool: Perforated Soft Cheese Knife

Cured Meats & Ham Slices

Serve paper-thin slices. If taking slices from a vacuum-sealed pack, let the package sit at room temperature for 20 minutes before separating the slices so they don't tear.

Recommended Tool: Thin Slicing Knife
